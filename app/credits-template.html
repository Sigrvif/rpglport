RPGLPort — Credits & Licenses

RPGLPort is a self-contained, offline tool that ports Windows RPG Maker MV/MZ games to Linux. It bundles the following software; each project's own license applies to its contribution. Full license texts are in the licenses/ folder where noted, and inside app/core/enigma/ for the EnigmaVB unpacker.
